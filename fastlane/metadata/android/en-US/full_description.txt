A tiny screen dimmer and warm (red) filter for Android TV boxes, projectors and TVs.

It draws a click-through overlay above everything, video included. Filter strength, brightness and colour temperature can be changed live, with no flash to full brightness:

* from the TV remote, in a settings screen that needs only the D-pad and OK, with a clearly visible focus highlight for TV boxes whose theme shows none
* from adb shell, e.g. <i>am start-foreground-service -n dev.redmoonbeam/.DimService --ei red 60 --ei bright 40 --ei temp 1100</i>
* from Home Assistant, through the Android Debug Bridge integration (example package and dashboard in the source repository)
* from a phone: scan the QR code shown on the TV and use the page in the phone browser, no app to install

A daily schedule (movie night, early to bed…) is applied by the TV itself, even with the phone off, and shown as a timeline at the bottom of the TV screen.

The last setting is restored early at boot. The service can only be controlled by the app itself and from adb shell, not by other apps.

Plain Java, about 55 KB, no trackers, no internet connections. The network permission is used only for the optional LAN remote (the app's HTTP server on port 8765, protected by a pairing key and off unless you enable it) and, in phone app mode, to connect to the TVs you paired on your local network.

The optional accessibility service is used only to draw the dimming overlay above everything on Android 12+; it reads no content.

The overlay permission can be granted with <i>adb shell appops set dev.redmoonbeam SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW allow</i>, or in Settings, Special app access, Display over other apps.
