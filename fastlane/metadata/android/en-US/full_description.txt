A tiny screen dimmer and warm (red) filter for Android TV boxes, projectors and TVs.

It draws a click-through overlay above everything, video included. Filter strength, brightness and colour temperature can be changed live, with no flash to full brightness:

* from the TV remote, in a settings screen that needs only the D-pad and OK, with a clearly visible focus highlight for TV boxes whose theme shows none
* from adb shell, e.g. <i>am start-foreground-service -n dev.overlaydimmer/.DimService --ei red 60 --ei bright 40 --ei temp 1100</i>
* from Home Assistant, through the Android Debug Bridge integration (example package and dashboard in the source repository)

The last setting is restored early at boot. The service can only be controlled by the app itself and from adb shell, not by other apps.

Plain Java, no libraries, no internet permission, about 20 KB.

The overlay permission can be granted with <i>adb shell appops set dev.overlaydimmer SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW allow</i>, or in Settings, Special app access, Display over other apps.
